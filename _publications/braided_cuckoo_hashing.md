We introduce braided cuckoo hashing, an open-addressed hash table supporting queries, insertions, and deletions in $$O(\log \varepsilon^{-1})$$ worst-case time with high probability at load factor up to $$1-\varepsilon$$. This improves the previous worst-case insertion and deletion bounds exponentially and disproves a conjectured tradeoff.
