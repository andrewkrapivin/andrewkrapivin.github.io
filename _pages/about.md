Welcome to my site! I'm Andrew Krapivin, a first-year PhD student in computer science at CMU, advised by [William Kuszmaul](https://sites.google.com/site/williamkuszmaul). Broadly, I am interested in developing theory, particulary of data structures, and finding new applications in which it can be useful. I have worked on improving the performance of hash tables at high load factors, on reducing the number of clauses in SAT formulas, on a computational model to capture out of order execution on modern CPUs, on comparing encoders and (reasoning) decoders from a theoretical perspective, and more.

I previously completed an MPhil in Advanced Computer Science at Cambridge University, funded by a [Churchill Scholarship](https://www.churchillscholarship.org/). Before that, I received a BS in mathematics and computer science from Rutgers University.

If you want to discuss anything, feel free to reach out!
